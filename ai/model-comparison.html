What the premium buys

gpt-5.6-terra costs 11× gpt-5.6-luna and bought one extra caught rule bug across 13 controls. Both overturned line-height-resolution independently and were right — that rule has been deleted, and deleting it raised out-of-sample coverage from 93.4% to 97.2%.

The models' most valuable output was not explanation. It was catching two rules that explained the wrong thing.

Mechanism yes, verdict no

The three p13-form-wizard buttons are byte-identical in every field a model sees. gpt-5-mini answered expected / expected / defect. luna and terra are each self-consistent — and flatly contradict each other.

Defect-vs-expected is not decidable from this evidence. The matcher abstains on 83% of labelled findings; the models should too.

Method. 27 findings pre-registered before any request: 13 one-per-rule controls, 7 matcher/owner disagreements, 7 findings no rule could explain. Every model saw byte-identical evidence, re-collected after maxWidth was added to the pipeline. Controls are scored by mechanism, judged by hand and recorded per cell in ai/model-comparison-scored.json; a model that named the same cause one level deeper counts as agreeing. Two overturns were verified against the evidence before being accepted.
Gaps. gpt-5-mini and gpt-5.6-terra each hit Tier-1 daily request limits on the last 4 findings, all in the lowest-priority bucket; the work was ordered so a limit would cost the least. Controls and disagreements are complete for all three. Playwright's WebKit is not Safari.
